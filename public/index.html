    How to play

    1. One round inflatable boat, one crew. Each player is a character lying on their belly on the boat's rim, bottom hanging out over the water. You can only stand on your own slice: all of it if you play alone, half for two, a third for three, a quarter for four.
    2. Move to choose where you lie. Push the stick toward the part of the boat you want. Your fart pushes the boat the opposite way, so go to the back to go forward. The arrow on the boat shows your push.
    3. Hold TOOT to charge, release to fart. Longer holds are bigger and cost more gas. Gas refills slowly. Keyboard: WASD or arrows to move, Space to toot.
    4. Too much farting makes your bum red and sore. A sore bum makes weaker farts until it cools down. Pace yourself.
    5. The river fights back. Rocks bump you. Spiky urchins, spiked rollers (from the left, the right and straight ahead) and purple spiked rails always hurt. Whirlpools drag you in and flush you out. Waterfalls fling you down and rattle everyone's gas.
    6. Share a hull. The crew has 5 hull points. At zero the boat sinks and restarts from the last flag. Stars: 3 for no sinking, 3 hits or fewer and beating par. 2 for at most one sinking within 1.6 times par. 1 for finishing.
